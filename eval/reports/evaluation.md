# Evaluation

136 series (1 total, 3 regions, 12 stores, 120 store x product) over 1092 days; 10% of store x product days sell nothing. Calibrated on 6 four-week blocks, then backtested on 8 more, each forecast made from data before its block only. Generated by `forecast evaluate`.

## Accuracy (WAPE, lower is better)

| method | total | region | store | store x product | MASE, store x product | bias, store x product |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| seasonal naive | 0.220 | 0.255 | 0.340 | 0.676 | 0.779 | -6.6% |
| Holt-Winters | 0.217 | 0.244 | 0.311 | 0.600 | 0.669 | -7.6% |
| GBM (base) | 0.155 | 0.190 | 0.251 | 0.431 | 0.514 | -7.5% |
| GBM bottom-up | 0.110 | 0.140 | 0.207 | 0.431 | 0.514 | -7.5% |
| GBM + WLS | 0.123 | 0.156 | 0.220 | 0.451 | 0.524 | -2.9% |
| GBM + MinT | 0.119 | 0.152 | 0.217 | 0.446 | 0.522 | -4.4% |

Forecasts that don't add up (share of volume): GBM base 9.71%, bottom-up 0e+00, MinT 0e+00.
